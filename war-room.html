Connect your clan

This page talks to a small local relay running on your own PC at localhost:8787, which holds your CoC and DeepSeek API keys and forwards requests on your behalf — browsers aren't allowed to attach those keys directly to cross-origin requests. Start it first: open a terminal in the folder with coc-local-proxy.js (after setting your keys in .env) and run node coc-local-proxy.js (or npm start). Leave that window open, then connect below.
